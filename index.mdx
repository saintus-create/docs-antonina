## Get Started in Three Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Encyclopedia">
    Head to the [Introduction](/introduction) for an overview of the encyclopedia's scope, editorial methodology, and how articles are structured.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search or Browse">
    Type any rhetorical term into the search bar for instant full-text results, or visit [Browse Entries](/browse/overview) to navigate alphabetically or by thematic category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dive Into an Entry">
    Each article includes a definition, historical context, key examples, and cross-references — giving you everything you need to understand a concept in depth.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  **Ask the AI assistant anything.** The built-in Claude 3.5 Sonnet chat assistant can answer questions about rhetorical concepts, compare theorists, suggest related entries, and help you apply rhetorical principles to your own writing — all without leaving the encyclopedia.
</Tip>
