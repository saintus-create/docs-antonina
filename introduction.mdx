# Encyclopedia of Rhetoric and Composition

The Encyclopedia of Rhetoric and Composition is a scholarly reference covering rhetorical theory and practice. Entries range from Aristotle and Cicero to digital discourse and feminist rhetoric, mapping the breadth of rhetoric as a discipline: its history, its key figures, its foundational terms, and its applications in law, literature, philosophy, politics, and everyday communication.

## Entry Types

The encyclopedia covers rhetoric in its widest sense — the oldest and broadest of the humanities, drawing on anthropology, linguistics, philosophy, and psychology. Entries are organized to serve readers at every level of familiarity with the field.

- **Identifications** — concise definitions of terms, figures, and concepts
- **Notes** — elaborated explanations that situate key topics in their rhetorical context
- **Essays** — in-depth explorations of major subjects, tracing historical development and scholarly debate
- **Articles** — full treatments that illuminate rhetoric's art and methodology with bibliography and cross-references
